Course Units

Ten teaching units in the EU Business School active-learning model. Each unit pairs a signature method — Presentation, Case Study, Role Play, Hackathon or Business Challenge — with a Bostelaar core message, a Thumbnail-Thinking framework gallery, a recent case, pre-reading, reflection, and a Council-of-Giants author Slibrary. Every framework opens as a thumbnail, a short definition and a downloadable PDF. Expand a unit to begin.

Framework Gallery

Thirty-nine interactive tools organised into ten Slibraries — one per unit — that follow the design thinking journey. Filter by Slibrary, then click any framework thumbnail to open its one-page PDF reference sheet (professional definition, who and why it was developed, how, when and by whom it is used, innovation relative to prior practice, related frameworks and Harvard sources).

Slibrary — The Thinkers Behind the Frameworks

A visual reference library of the 19 thinkers behind the frameworks in this course. Each card is a Slibrary — built in the JD Meier "Author Distilled" tradition — showing the why, the foundation, the frameworks, the operationalisation, the limitations and the enduring impact of one author. Click any card to expand the full profile; each also downloads as a PDF.
